The complete GTA 6 trophy guide: all 55 trophies with a plain-English guide for each one, cited against PowerPyx, Push Square and SixShore at launch, on a checklist tracker that remembers what you have earned. A six-stage roadmap runs story first, then the 100% checklist, then the stranger strands and the last clean-up jobs to the platinum. Kind and grade filters, search, and a sort control (trophy order, roadmap stage, name, grade) sit above the list, with a spoiler-safe mode that hides unearned story trophy names, a detail view with the full guide and sources for every trophy, export/import of your progress, and a copy of everything still left to earn. The one trophy that can be missed in Chapter 1 (Positive Mental Attitude) is flagged wherever it appears, and the six trophies whose methods are still unconfirmed at launch say so plainly instead of guessing. No online trophies, one playthrough, about 200 hours to platinum.

node serve.ts
