⚠ One trophy can be missed — read this first

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) is the only trophy PowerPyx flags as possibly missable: the first meditation is Lucia on the balcony at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, the morning after the mission Down to Earth. Talk to Lucia in the kitchen, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept. It is not confirmed whether that session counts — doing it is the safe choice, because there is no mission replay.

Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off on your trophy run.
